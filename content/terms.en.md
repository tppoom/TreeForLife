## 3. Botanical Care Disclaimer

### 3.1 Thai 3-Season Climate & Pot Material Calibrations
Our care algorithm calculates watering and feeding intervals based on statistical climate patterns across Thailand's 3 seasons:
* **Hot Season (March – May):** Higher temperatures and rapid evaporation.
* **Rainy Season (June – October):** Elevated humidity requiring caution against root rot.
* **Cool Season (November – February):** Slower metabolic growth and adjusted watering frequencies.

Intervals are calibrated against five pot materials: Terracotta, Plastic, Glazed Ceramic, Raw Concrete, and Hanging Baskets.

### 3.2 Household Microclimates & Natural Variations
Plant health and moisture uptake depend heavily on each household's unique microclimate, including:
* Direct vs. filtered sunlight duration and seasonal sun angle shifts.
* Air conditioning usage and ambient indoor relative humidity.
* Physical aeration, drainage, and porosity of your chosen potting mix.

> **Important Horticultural Guidance:** All schedules and recommendations are general guidelines. Plant parents should always observe actual moisture levels (e.g. checking soil 1–2 inches deep with a clean finger or moisture probe). TreeForLife cannot guarantee plant survival or blooming due to biological and environmental factors beyond our control.
