## 1. Introduction & Commitment to Data Protection

At **TreeForLife**, we are deeply committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our customers and visitors. In compliance with Thailand's **Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (PDPA)**, this Privacy Policy explains clearly and transparently what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is processed and secured, retention periods, and your statutory rights as a data subject.

---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect (Data Collection Disclosure)

TreeForLife operates strictly under the principle of **Data Minimization**, collecting only the minimal information required to compute intelligent care schedules tailored to authentic Thai microclimates:

### 2.1 Guest Mode (Unauthenticated Visitors)
You can explore our boutique plant catalog and manage plant care routines without mandatory account creation. In Guest Mode, we store:
* **Guest Token:** An anonymous random unique identifier (UUID v4) stored locally on your device via browser LocalStorage to link your virtual garden and watering tasks.
* **Plant Records:** Custom plant entries added to your personal garden (My Garden), including custom plant nicknames, acquisition dates, pot sizes, pot materials (5 distinct materials), and home placement zones.
* **Care Schedules & Activity Logs:** Watering due dates, completed tasks, snooze events, and care action timestamps.
* **Inquiry Reference Codes:** Consultation ticket identifiers (e.g., `TFL-YYMM-XXXX`), botanical problem descriptions, sunlight contexts, and plant photos submitted to request specialized nursery guidance.

### 2.2 Registered User Accounts (Optional Sign-In)
When you connect an account or sign in:
* **Account Credentials:** User ID, display name, verified email address, and optional profile image.
* **Multi-Device Synchronization:** Automatic conversion of local guest garden records into your permanent account database.

## 3. Purposes of Processing Personal Data

We process personal data solely for lawful and transparent purposes:
1. **Intelligent 3-Season Care Scheduling (Smart Care Engine):** Factoring botanical species requirements, Thai microclimates (Hot, Rainy, and Cool seasons), and pot permeability to deliver accurate watering intervals.
2. **Botanical Consultation Fulfillment:** Referencing care logs and inquiry codes to provide personalized horticultural advice via our LINE Official Account.
3. **Platform Integrity & Security:** Enforcing rate limits against abuse, maintaining service uptime, and collecting strictly anonymized aggregate usage metrics.

## 5. Data Retention Policy

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes set out in this policy:

* **Inactive Guest Records:** Garden records associated with an inactive Guest Token without any user interaction for **180 consecutive days** are automatically purged from our primary database.
* **Notifications Log:** Kept for **180 days** to ensure reliable message dispatch audits, after which logs are permanently deleted.
* **Consultation Inquiries:** Retained for horticultural history, but stripped of all personally identifying attributes (Anonymization) whenever a user requests account deletion.
* **Self-Service Instant Erasure:** Guest users may trigger "Clear Data on This Device" anytime in My Garden to erase local storage and linked server records immediately.

## 6. Cookies and Local Storage Technologies

TreeForLife utilizes only **Strictly Necessary Technical Storage** essential for website functionality:
* `tfl_guest_token`: Maintains your virtual garden state without requiring account sign-in.
* `tfl_theme`: Preserves your color theme preference (Light or Dark mode).
* `tfl_locale`: Remembers your preferred display language (Thai or English).

We **do not use advertising or behavioral tracking cookies** (no Meta Pixel, no Google Ads tracking). Because no non-essential tracking mechanisms are present, no intrusive cookie consent banner is needed under Thai PDPA guidelines.

## 7. Your Statutory Rights under Thailand PDPA (Data Subject Rights)

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562, you hold the following rights:
1. **Right of Access:** Request confirmation and access to a copy of your personal data stored with us.
2. **Right to Data Portability:** Download your full garden records, plant care logs, and account history in an open, structured JSON format.
3. **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** Request permanent deletion and destruction of your personal data and account records.
4. **Right to Rectification:** Update and correct inaccurate or incomplete plant records and profile information at any time.
5. **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Revoke previously granted consents for notifications or data processing.
6. **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** Lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) if you believe your statutory rights have been infringed.
